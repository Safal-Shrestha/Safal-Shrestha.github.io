02 / How I think

The interesting part is usually why.

01

Understand the problem.

Before reaching for a stack, I want to understand the actual problem, its constraints and who or what the system is serving.

02

Make the system visible.

I like decomposing complicated problems into components, boundaries, flows and responsibilities. Then I ask how they interact.

03

Question the decision.

Architecture is trade-offs. A choice that made sense six months ago might not make sense today. I want my work to show that evolution.

An evolving engineering journal

What I learn should change what I build.

Case studies, architecture decisions, failed assumptions and retrospectives will live here as I learn more.

04 / About

Still figuring it out.
That's the point.

I started with a simple fascination with computers, then began coding random things just to see what was possible. Backend development pulled me toward the question behind the feature: what makes the whole thing work?

Along the way I explored AI, design, photography, video, events, MUN, Rotaract and AIESEC. They looked like separate interests until I noticed the pattern. I keep wanting to understand systems: how they work, why they work, where they break and how they can be improved.

I'm now building toward systems engineering, starting where I am: writing software, learning the fundamentals and deliberately revisiting the decisions I make.
